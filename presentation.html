Abstract

VoxForensics is a state-of-the-art forensic audio analysis application designed to detect deepfake and synthetic speech in real-time. With the emergence of neural voice synthesis and diffusion vocoders, distinguishing genuine human voices from AI-generated audio has become virtually impossible for human listeners alone.

The application extracts 45 acoustic audio features (spectral, cepstral, temporal, and pitch parameters) from audio inputs and classifies them using a trained Random Forest Machine Learning model with calibrated confidence scoring and real/fake probability distributions.

Working Mechanism — 45 Features

45 Acoustic Features Analyzed

The ML pipeline normalizes incoming audio to 16 kHz mono and calculates exactly 45 acoustic features using librosa and soundfile:

MFCCs 1–13 (26 Features)

Mean and Standard Deviation for 13 Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients capturing vocal tract resonance.

Spectral Profile (8 Features)

Mean and Std for Spectral Centroid, Spectral Rolloff, Spectral Bandwidth, and Spectral Flatness.

Temporal & Energy (4 Features)

Zero-Crossing Rate (mean & std) and RMS Energy (mean & std) quantifying acoustic power and noisiness.

Chroma & Rhythm (3 Features)

Chroma STFT (mean & std) and estimated Speech Tempo capturing harmonic patterns.

Fundamental Pitch Statistics (4 Features)

Fundamental frequency (F0) tracking via probabilistic YIN (pYIN): Pitch Mean, Pitch Std, Pitch Min, and Pitch Max to measure natural vs. synthetic intonation.

Exact Feature Vector Match

The 45 extracted metrics map directly to the feature vector required by the serialized Random Forest Classifier (deepfake_detector.joblib), verified by model.n_features_in_ = 45.

Classification & Feature Comparison

Real Voice vs. Synthetic Voice Signatures

Feature Group Count Authentic Human Speech AI-Generated Synthetic Speech
MFCCs 1–13 (Mean & Std) 26 Smooth, organic physiological transitions Subtle coefficient clustering and vocoder harmonics
Spectral Features 8 Natural high-frequency rolloff, tonal resonance Elevated spectral flatness, artificial noise floor
Zero-Crossing & RMS 4 Dynamic breath decay and energy contours Unnaturally uniform frame-by-frame energy distribution
Chroma & Tempo 3 Spontaneous cadence and natural pauses Mechanically rigid temporal rhythm
Pitch Statistics (F0) 4 Expressive pitch variability and natural micro-fluctuations Monotone flatness or repetitive pitch micro-tremors

Ensemble Decision Trees

The Random Forest ensemble aggregates predictions across individual decision trees to compute un-biased class probabilities (predict_proba), returning calibrated confidence scores.

Authentication & Security Governance

Enterprise Authentication Flow

  • Firebase Authentication: Mandatory email verification before dashboard entry.
  • Application-Level Email OTP: Cryptographic random 6-digit code via crypto.getRandomValues.
  • Salted SHA-256 Hashing: Plaintext OTP is never stored; verified via salted SHA-256 hash.
  • Session Restrictions: 10-minute expiry, 60-second cooldown, max 5 attempts.
  • Show/Hide Password: Interactive eye icon for secure credential input.

Administrative Controls & Safeguards

  • Role Management: Real-time promote and demote capabilities in Firestore.
  • Callable Cloud Function: deleteUserAccount in Node 20 with Firebase Admin SDK.
  • Self-Deletion Prevention: Administrators cannot delete their own active account.
  • Primary Admin Immunity: Protected admin@voxforensics.com cannot be deleted.

Privacy & Data Governance

Voice Data Lifecycle

Ephemeral Audio Handling:

  • Audio is held temporarily in RAM / isolated temp file only for feature extraction
  • Temporary audio files are deleted immediately after feature calculation
  • No audio recordings are permanently stored on servers
  • Audio Object URLs revoked in browser cleanup hooks

Persistent Metadata Governance:

  • Only analytical metadata (filename, duration, scores) saved in Firestore
  • Users retain full control to delete scan history at any time
  • User profiles can be deleted safely via Admin Dashboard
